# Help Desk Intern Work Sample

**Five customer scenarios | Suggested replies, priorities, tags and product improvements**

---

**Message 1: "I applied to 14 internships last month and heard back from none. Is my account broken? Should I stop applying?"**

### Reply to the user

> I understand how frustrating this can be. It doesn't necessarily mean your account is broken, as employers have different response timelines and may contact only shortlisted candidates. I can verify your application history if you share the relevant role names or IDs. Meanwhile, keep applying to suitable roles and tailoring your CV.

**Priority:** This week  
**Reason:** There is no sign of an immediate account failure or safety risk; first verify submission records.

**Tag:** `applications` / `application-status`

**Product improvement:** Add an application tracker showing submission confirmation, current status, last updated date and a clear note that employers may not respond to every applicant.

---

**Message 2: "I can't sign in. Please change the email on my account to this new one, I don't have the old one any more. My name is the same."**

### Reply to the user

> I can help you recover access, but we cannot change your email based on a matching name alone. For your account's security, we first need to verify your identity. Please use the official account-recovery process or contact our support team through the website. Never share your password or OTP. Once your ownership is verified, we can help update your email and restore access.

**Priority:** Today  
**Reason:** The user is locked out, and an unverified email change could let someone take over the account.

**Tag:** `account-access` / `identity-verification`

**Product improvement:** Provide a secure account-recovery flow with ownership checks, clear status updates and a verified email-change process.

---

**Message 3: "Your site says the role closed but the company's own page still says apply by Friday. Which is right?"**

### Reply to the user

> Thanks for flagging the mismatch. The employer’s own careers page may have the most current information, but I don’t want to promise that the vacancy is still accepting applications until it is checked. Please share the role link and company name. We’ll verify the listing with the employer or its official application page, then correct our status and let you know. If the official page still accepts applications, follow its instructions and deadline; if it no longer does, please don’t rely on the older platform listing.

**Priority:** Today  
**Reason:** A stale status can cause someone to miss a real opportunity or spend time on a closed role.

**Tag:** `job-listing` / `role-status`

**Product improvement:** Automatically recheck external application links and show a "last verified" timestamp, with a report-mismatch option for users.

---

**Message 4: "An employer from your site messaged me on WhatsApp. They said I am selected and must pay Rs 1,500 for training before I join. Is this normal?"**

### Reply to the user

> Please do not pay the ₹1,500 or share banking details, OTPs or identity documents. A demand for money to secure selection is a serious scam warning sign; treat the message as suspicious unless independently verified through the company’s official website or contact details. Do not use phone numbers or links supplied in that WhatsApp message. Please send support a screenshot with your personal details and sensitive information hidden, plus the role link and the sender’s claimed company name. We’ll review the listing and escalate it for investigation.

**Priority:** Urgent  
**Reason:** The user may lose money or disclose sensitive information imminently; prevent payment first and escalate the report.

**Tag:** `safety` / `suspected-recruitment-scam`

**Product improvement:** Add a prominent job-scam reporting flow and safety warning on listings explaining that applicants should never pay to obtain a job; review and remove suspicious employers promptly.

---

**Message 5: "I uploaded my CV as a Word file and it says it can't read it. I don't have a PDF. What do I do?"**

### Reply to the user

> Sorry about the trouble. You may not need to recreate your CV. First, check that the Word file is in `.doc` or `.docx` format, opens normally and isn’t password-protected. If you have Microsoft Word, choose **File → Save As** (or **Export**) → **PDF**, then upload the PDF. If you use Google Docs, open the file and choose **File → Download → PDF Document**. If neither option is available, tell us which device and app you’re using and we’ll suggest another route. Please don’t send your CV in a public chat.

**Priority:** This week  
**Reason:** The user has a workable alternative and the issue does not appear time-critical unless an application deadline is imminent.

**Tag:** `CV-upload` / `file-format`

**Product improvement:** Accept `.doc` and `.docx` files reliably, explain supported formats beside the upload control, and provide a clear conversion/help link when parsing fails.

---

## Which message would I handle first?

I would handle **message 4 first**. The user is facing a potentially active recruitment scam and could lose money or expose sensitive information, so the immediate response should stop payment and protect their data.

I would then escalate the listing and sender for investigation. Message 2 would be the next priority because an unsafe email change could compromise an account.
